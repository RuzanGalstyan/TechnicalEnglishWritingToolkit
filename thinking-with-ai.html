AI ethics in writing

Using AI
as a Critic

Stop asking “can you write this?” and start asking “what is wrong with this?” Twenty exercises.

Designed by Ruzana Galstyan-Sargsyan · Universitat Politècnica de València

Why this matters

Ask a tool to write your paragraph and you have outsourced the thinking. Ask it to attack your paragraph and you have a reader available at midnight who never gets bored. The second is more useful, almost always permitted, and much harder to do well.

Outsourcing"Write my discussion section."You get sentences you cannot defend and learn nothing.
Sharpening"What is my discussion section missing? Do not rewrite it."You get objections you can check, and the writing stays yours.

Four moves

MovePrompt
Adversarial readingRead this as a sceptical examiner. List the three weakest claims and say why. Do not rewrite.
SteelmanningMake the strongest case for the approach I rejected. Assume the person making it is competent.
Explain it backSummarise my argument in three sentences. Do not improve it — I want to see whether it came across.
InterrogationAsk me five questions a marker would ask about this method. One at a time.
Explain-it-back is the quiet standout. Unclear writing is far more common than wrong writing and much harder to see in your own work. If the restatement is wrong, your writing is unclear — and you found out without a reader having to be polite.

The pattern

Instead ofAsk
Write my discussion section.What is my discussion section missing?
Improve this paragraph.Which sentence is weakest, and why?
Is this good?Give me the three strongest objections to this.
Summarise this paper.Here is my summary — what did I miss?

Every right-hand version requires you to have done the work first. That is not a coincidence.

Two failure modes to watch

They are agreeable. Ask “is this good?” and you will usually be told it is, with three compliments. That is a property of the tool, not evidence about your work. Treat unqualified praise as a non-answer.

Fabricated citations cannot be spotted by eye. Some have visible tells — a journal that does not match the field, an implausible page count. Most do not. A reference that looks perfect is not verified; it is unfalsified. The only method is to search for it and open it.

Form your own view first. If you always ask what is wrong before deciding what you think, you are outsourcing judgement rather than text — harder to notice, and worse for you.
