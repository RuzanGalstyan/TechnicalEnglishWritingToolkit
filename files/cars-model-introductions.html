Writing an
Introduction

Three moves take a reader from your field to your gap. Twenty exercises, including the move students skip.

Designed by Ruzana Galstyan-Sargsyan · Universitat Politècnica de València

Why this matters

Your introduction has one job: to make the reader believe your work was worth doing. Three moves do it — establish the field, name what is missing, say what you did about it.

No reason to exist"Cybersecurity is important. Many studies have examined it. This report examines cybersecurity in small firms."Nothing is wrong with any sentence, but the reader has no idea why the report exists. The middle move is missing.
Necessary work"…However, most of this work has focused on large plants. Little attention has been paid to small manufacturers. This study therefore examines…"Gap, then aim. Now the work has to be done by someone.

Three moves

MoveJobLanguage
1 TerritoryShow the area matters and say what is knownIn recent years… · X is widely used in… · Several studies have examined…
2 NicheName what is missing or unresolvedHowever… · Little attention has been paid to… · It remains unclear whether…
3 OccupyingState your aim, and how the text is organisedThis study therefore examines… · The report is organised as follows…
Move 2 is the one students skip, and it is the sentence that makes your work necessary rather than merely additional. It almost always starts with a contrast signal: However, Nevertheless, Despite this.

Gap language, cautious to bold

StrengthPhrasing
CautiousRelatively few studies have considered…
NeutralLittle attention has been paid to…
FirmerThis aspect has not yet been investigated.
BoldTo date, no study has addressed…

Prefer the cautious end. A marker who knows of one counter-example will conclude your literature search was inadequate.

Tense

  • Present simple for the current situation: Energy demand is rising.
  • Present perfect for previous research: Several studies have examined…
  • Past simple for one specific study: Park (2022) measured…
The coherence test. Read your gap sentence and your aim sentence one after the other. They should sound like a question and its answer. If not, rewrite the gap.

For Spanish speakers

From SpanishEnglish
In the last years, interest has grown.In recent years, interest has grown.
This work pretends to analyse…This work aims to analyse…
It is important to remark that…It should be noted that…
The investigation of Park (2022) shows…The research by Park (2022) shows… / Park (2022) shows…
Nowadays, nowadays, nowadays…Vary it: currently, at present, in recent years.
Investigación is research, not investigation. In English an investigation usually means a police or disciplinary inquiry. And research is uncountable: research shows, never researches show or a research.

The moves describe, they do not prescribe. Real introductions cycle back through Moves 1 and 2 several times, and engineering introductions run shorter than social science ones. Read two introductions from your own field's journals and check the pattern against what you see.
