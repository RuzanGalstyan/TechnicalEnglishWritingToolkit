About these tools

Each tool opens on practice. You answer, it marks itself, and it explains every answer — right or wrong. A reference tab holds the rules if you get stuck, and several tools end with a scaffold or draft you can copy into your own work.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is saved and nothing is sent anywhere, so your scores reset when you reload. All the tools print cleanly if you would rather work on paper.

Several tools include a strand marked For Spanish speakers, covering errors that come from Spanish rather than from English. If Spanish is not your first language, you can skip those items.

The Technical writing tools are adapted in part from Last, S. (2019). Technical Writing Essentials. University of Victoria, licensed CC BY 4.0.

The AI ethics in writing tools are based on Cheng, Calhoun, & Reedy. 2025. Artificial intelligence-assisted academic writing: recommendations for ethical use. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41077-025-00350-6 — open access.

The format of this hub was inspired by Hamid Guedra's HTML Tools at LAB University of Applied Sciences.

Examples throughout — studies, companies, faults and figures — were written for teaching and are not real. Do not cite them.
