APA 7
Referencing

In-text citations, reference lists, and the four rules that changed from APA 6. Twenty exercises.

Designed by Ruzana Galstyan-Sargsyan · Universitat Politècnica de València

Why this matters

Referencing is the one part of your report a marker can check in thirty seconds without reading a word of your argument. Get it wrong and it costs marks before they reach your engineering.

APA 6 habits"Smith, J., Khan, V., & Zhang, H. (2019)… New York, NY: Springer. doi:10.1000/xyz"Three faults at once: authors listed in full, publisher location, and the old doi: prefix. All three were correct in APA 6 and are wrong now.
APA 7"Smith et al. (2019)… Springer. https://doi.org/10.1000/xyz"Et al. from the first citation, no location, DOI as a link.

Ampersand or "and"? Use & inside brackets, and in running text. (Park & Silva, 2022) but Park and Silva (2022) found…
Three or more authors: et al. from the very first citation. This is the change that catches people who learned APA 6, where you listed up to five names the first time. Et al. is not italicised, and the full stop belongs on al. — it is an abbreviation of alii.

Four things APA 7 changed

  • No publisher location. Just Springer, not New York, NY: Springer.
  • Up to 20 authors listed in full. For 21 or more, list the first 19, then an ellipsis, then the final author — with no ampersand before it.
  • DOIs are links. https://doi.org/10.xxxx, with no doi: prefix.
  • No "Retrieved from" before a URL, unless you also give a retrieval date for a page that changes.

Capitalisation catches everyone

Article and book titles use sentence case — only the first word, the first word after a colon, and proper nouns. Journal titles use Title Case. The journal title and volume number are italicised; the issue number in brackets is not.

Afirmar is not always affirm. In English, affirm is formal and slightly legal. For reporting a source, use states, argues, reports or suggests — see Choosing Your Citation Verb.

Check which style your programme requires. Many engineering programmes use IEEE numbered citations rather than APA. Some require APA 7. Your assignment brief or your TFG regulations decide, not this page — and if your brief says IEEE, none of the rules here apply.
About the examples. The authors, journals and DOIs in these exercises were invented for practice. Do not cite them, and do not test the links.
