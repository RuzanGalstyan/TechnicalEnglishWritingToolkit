Why this matters

This is the shape your TFG probably takes: you compare options against criteria and recommend one. The whole document exists to support a decision somebody else has to make.

No decision"Both options have advantages and disadvantages. Option A is cheaper but Option B is more efficient."True, and useless. The reader still has to decide, which is what they asked you to do.
A decision"Option B is recommended. Its higher capital cost is recovered within 4.2 years, and it meets the 2030 efficiency target that Option A does not."A choice, the criterion that decided it, and the trade-off acknowledged.

Three related report types

TypeQuestion it answers
FeasibilityCan this be done, and should it be?
RecommendationWhich of these options should we choose?
EvaluationHow well is the existing thing performing?
They share one structure, because all three compare something against criteria and reach a judgement. A TFG is usually a feasibility or recommendation report wearing an academic coat.

Typical contents

SectionJob
Executive summaryThe recommendation, up front. Written last, read first
IntroductionThe problem and who needs it solved
BackgroundWhat the reader needs to follow the comparison
CriteriaWhat "better" means here — stated before the options
OptionsEach described on the same terms
ComparisonOptions against criteria, usually a table
ConclusionsWhat the comparison shows
RecommendationWhat to do, and by when

Criteria come first

State your criteria before you describe the options, and say where they came from — a standard, a client requirement, a budget limit. Criteria invented after you looked at the options are how a report ends up justifying a decision that was already made.

Weighted objectives

When criteria matter unequally, weight them. Give each a weight, score each option, multiply, total. The value is not the arithmetic — it is that the reader can see your judgement and disagree with a specific number.

State the weights honestly. If cost matters three times as much as aesthetics to your client, say so. A weighting chosen to produce the answer you wanted is visible to anyone who changes one number.

Conclusions and recommendations are different

ConclusionRecommendation
Option B meets all four criteria; Option A fails on efficiency.Adopt Option B, with installation before the 2027 heating season.

A conclusion says what your analysis shows. A recommendation says what someone should do. Students routinely stop at the conclusion, and the report never answers the question it was written for.

Language of recommending

StrengthPhrasing
StrongIt is recommended that Option B be adopted.
StandardOption B should be adopted.
TentativeOption B could be considered, subject to confirmation of the maintenance cost.

For Spanish speakers

From SpanishEnglish
In base to these criteria…On the basis of these criteria… / Based on these criteria…
Option B is the more convenient.Option B is the more suitable.
We recommend to adopt Option B.We recommend adopting Option B. / We recommend that Option B be adopted.
Option A presents a major cost.Option A has a higher cost.
Both options are valid, but…Both options are technically viable, but…
Conveniente is not convenient. It means suitable, advisable or appropriate. English convenient means easy or handy — not a criterion an engineer should be recommending on.
