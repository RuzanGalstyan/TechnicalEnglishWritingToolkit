Active or
Passive?

Nobody told you the real rule. It is not "avoid the passive" — it is "start the sentence with what it is about." Twenty exercises.

Designed by Ruzana Galstyan-Sargsyan · Universitat Politècnica de València

Why this matters

You were probably told to avoid the passive. In engineering that advice is wrong. The real question is not active or passive — it is what should this sentence start with?

Wrong tool"We heated the samples to 180 °C."In a Method section the reader is following the samples, not you. Naming yourself adds a variable that does not belong.
Right tool"The samples were heated to 180 °C."The sample is the subject. The procedure works whoever runs it.
Hiding"It was decided that the supplier would be changed."Decided by whom? Here the passive conceals something the reader needs.

One question, not one rule

Ask: what is this sentence about? Put that first. The voice follows automatically.

SectionUsual choiceExample
MethodPassiveThe sensor was calibrated before each run.
Results (figures)ActiveFigure 3 shows a sharp rise.
Results (findings)EitherOutput fell by 12%. / A 12% fall was recorded.
DiscussionActiveThese findings suggest progressive fouling.
InstructionsImperativePress the reset button.
Process descriptionPassiveThe water is filtered through sand.
Use the passive when the doer is irrelevant, unknown, or obvious — or when the thing acted on is what your paragraph is about.
Avoid the passive when it hides someone who matters. It was decided, mistakes were made, the report was not submitted — a reader notices the missing agent, and so does a marker.

Keep the agent when it carries information

Cut the agentKeep the agent

The samples were weighed by us.The standard was published by ISO.

Two traps

Not every passive-looking verb is passive. The system is made up of four units is fine. The system is consisted of four units is wrong — consist has no passive.

Long passives get heavy. If a sentence has two passives and a nominalisation, rewrite it: An investigation of the causes was carried out → We investigated the causes, or The causes were investigated.

For Spanish speakers

From SpanishEnglish
Se midió la temperatura → It measured the temperature.The temperature was measured.
Se puede observar que… → It can observe that…It can be seen that… / Figure 3 shows that…
The system is consisted of four units.The system consists of four units.
The results were obtained a 12% increase.A 12% increase was obtained. / The results show a 12% increase.
The se problem. Spanish impersonal se maps onto the English passive, not onto it + active verb. Se analizaron las muestras → The samples were analysed.
